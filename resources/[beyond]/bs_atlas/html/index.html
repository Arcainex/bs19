Click a cell on the map to inspect it. A cell is 50m x 50m and is the unit the far field counts in.

EDIT THIS CELL

Every one of these changes the world immediately and is printed to the server console with your name on it (from the web panel: as "web panel key holder" - the panel key does not identify a person).

BASELINE is what the cell regrows towards. COUNT is what is standing there now.

A LOCKED cell is skipped by regrowth and migration. CLEAR empties the count and starts the grace period - it does not despawn zombies that are already live peds in front of a player.

MAP POINTS FOR OUR MAP

The density map was drawn for stock GTA V. Walk to a place on The Apocalypse Project, stand in the middle of it, and drop a point. Higher density = more zombies around it. Points are saved to bs_zombies/data/atlas_points.json and win over config_atlas.lua.
