Cinematic camera
- - -
Smoothing values are time constants in seconds - how long the camera takes to cover most of the distance to what you are asking for. 0 is instant. Higher is heavier.
Mouse wheel changes this while flying. Low numbers are a long lens: flat, compressed, hard to hold steady. High numbers are wide and exaggerate every movement.
GTA's depth of field is coarse. It reads well on a wide shot with a clear foreground and badly on anything mid-distance.
Only you see it, and only while you are flying — your own filter comes back when you land. Mine leaves whatever you picked in Settings alone.
Slow motion. Path playback is measured in game time, so a slower scale gives you more frames of the same move rather than less of it.
Fly to a position, drop a keyframe, fly to the next one. Playback curves through every keyframe at a constant speed you could not hold by hand - and gives you the same move on every take.
