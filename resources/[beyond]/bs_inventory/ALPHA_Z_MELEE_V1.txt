Alpha-Z melee inventory integration
- Bottle / WEAPON_BOTTLE
- Pool Cue / WEAPON_POOLCUE
- Golf Club / WEAPON_GOLFCLUB
- Stone Hatchet / WEAPON_STONE_HATCHET
- Antique Cavalry Dagger / WEAPON_DAGGER
Each item starts at 200 durability and loses 1 only on a reported successful melee damage event.
At 0 durability the exact inventory copy is removed and the weapon is disarmed.
