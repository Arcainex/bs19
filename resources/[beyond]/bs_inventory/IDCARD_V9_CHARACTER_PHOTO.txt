Permanent character passport photo added.
- First photo per charId is stored in MySQL table bs_identity_photos.
- Automatic capture after spawn if the character has no stored photo.
- Existing characters get their photo after the update on first spawn/open.
- Photo is made from a local ped clone: hat/helmet, glasses and mask are removed.
- Player's real visible ped is not changed when clone capture works.
- Stored photo is reused forever and is sent when showing the ID to another player.
- To deliberately retake a photo, delete that char_id row from bs_identity_photos.
