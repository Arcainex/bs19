BS19 v1.1.0 — How to update

v1.1.0 is a large update with new resources and four database migrations.
On a server you never edited, a clean reinstall is the easiest path. On a
server you changed, follow these steps.

1. Stop the server and back up its folder and database.
2. Download v1.1.0 into a separate folder. Do not run the fresh-install recipe
   against an existing server, and do not extract the download over your files.
3. Copy the new resources into resources/[beyond] (and resources/[compat],
   resources/[prototypes]): bs_bodycam, bs_darkzone, bs_fighting, bs_fpdebug,
   bs_missions, bs_parkour, bs_pass, bs_survivor, bs_survivor_link, bs_voice,
   qbx_core and the three bs_attachment_* model packs.
4. For resources you never edited, take the v1.1.0 copy. For edited ones,
   compare your copy with v1.1.0 and merge the changes. Keep a backup.
5. Compare your server.cfg with the v1.1.0 one and add the new ensure lines in
   the same order. bs_voice replaces pma-voice. bs_esx_bridge is no longer
   started by default; keep it if you rely on it.
6. Keep your credentials, F1 profiles, custom resources and database. The new
   migrations (045-048) run on their own when bs_core starts. 048 renames the
   old backpack items and keeps their contents.
7. Restart the full server. If a problem appears, stop it and restore your
   backup.
